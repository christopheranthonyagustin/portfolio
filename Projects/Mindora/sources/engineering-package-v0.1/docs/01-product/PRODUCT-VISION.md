# Mindora Product Vision

## Product definition

Mindora is a **Personal Intelligence & Knowledge Operating System (KOS)**: a persistent intelligence layer that learns from a user's authorized knowledge sources, understands projects and activities, remembers what matters, and supplies relevant context to AI models and agents.

## Problem: AI amnesia

Users repeatedly explain their background, preferences, projects, systems, decisions, technical environment and current progress to AI tools. Context is fragmented across conversations, documents, repositories and applications. This creates repetitive work, lost decisions, inconsistent outputs and wasted tokens.

## Product principle

> The AI should adapt to the user's accumulated knowledge, not require the user to repeatedly adapt to the AI's limited memory.

## Product outcomes

1. Establish a grounded baseline profile and knowledge map.
2. Learn continuously from authorized projects, documents, conversations, activities and technical systems.
3. Preserve source provenance, confidence, history and corrections.
4. Connect related knowledge across people, projects, systems, decisions and events.
5. Retrieve only the context relevant to the current task.
6. Support multiple AI models and agents through a controlled shared knowledge layer.
7. Keep the user in control of consequential changes and access.

## Core capability domains

### Personal knowledge
Identity and profile, professional history, skills, capabilities, preferences, goals, qualifications and career history.

### Continuous learning
Projects, ideas, plans, conversations, emails, meetings, documents, decisions, tasks, outcomes, corrections and evolving responsibilities.

### Technical knowledge intelligence
Applications, repositories, code, languages, frameworks, dependencies, models, agents, prompts, workflows, infrastructure, cloud, databases, APIs, integrations, automation, CI/CD, deployments, configurations, incidents and technical decisions.

### Intelligent context engine
Intent classification, domain routing, relevance ranking, evidence-aware retrieval, context assembly, token optimization, uncertainty detection and cross-project connections.

### Knowledge governance
Provenance, versioning, confidence, verification, authorization, sensitive-data handling, audit, retention, deletion, backup and recovery.

## Initial practical domain

CareerOps is the first practical domain for validating persistent context and operational intelligence. The initial operational use cases include career profile continuity, job discovery, recruiter and company intelligence, email intelligence, application tracking, interview preparation and controlled follow-up workflows.

CareerOps is a starting domain, not the full product definition.

## Out of scope for the initial foundation

- Training a foundation model from scratch.
- Unrestricted autonomous access to all personal accounts and files.
- Automatic persistence of unverified inferences as facts.
- Replacing every existing application before auditing its value.
- Building every connector before proving the core memory and context workflow.
