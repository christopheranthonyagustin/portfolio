# AceCom Enterprise IT Infrastructure

## Enterprise IT Infrastructure Operations & Governance

> **Duration:** 2015 – 2024
>
> Internal enterprise IT infrastructure supporting warehouse operations, enterprise resource planning (ERP), logistics applications, corporate networking, and business-critical systems.

---

# Project Overview

AceCom operates a logistics and distribution business that relies on multiple enterprise applications supporting warehouse operations, order management, inventory management, and corporate services.

In addition to software engineering responsibilities, I was responsible for the day-to-day operation, governance, and coordination of the company's internal IT infrastructure. My responsibilities included infrastructure monitoring, enterprise application administration, disaster recovery planning, operational documentation, vendor coordination, and production support.

Rather than implementing infrastructure independently, I managed the operational lifecycle of enterprise systems by coordinating with infrastructure, networking, ERP, and security vendors to ensure system availability, reliability, and business continuity.

---

# Business Challenges

The internal infrastructure needed to support:

- Enterprise Resource Planning (Microsoft Dynamics NAV)
- Warehouse Management System (WMS)
- Order Management System (OMS)
- Internal business applications
- Corporate authentication
- Database services
- Corporate networking
- Business continuity
- Disaster Recovery (DR)

The environment required proactive monitoring, operational governance, preventive maintenance, vendor coordination, and documented recovery procedures to minimize operational downtime.

---

# Solution

Managed the operational readiness of the company's enterprise infrastructure through standardized operational procedures, infrastructure governance, preventive maintenance, and vendor coordination.

The environment consisted of Windows Server infrastructure supporting Active Directory, Microsoft SQL Server, Microsoft Dynamics NAV, enterprise applications, corporate networking, and security appliances.
